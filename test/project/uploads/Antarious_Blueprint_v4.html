01 · The Problem

The B2B GTM stack is broken.

Most B2B marketing and sales teams work across 17+ disconnected tools, spend a third of their time on coordination rather than execution, and still cannot tell which campaign actually closed a deal. Antarious was built to fix all three.

Problem 01

17 Tools, Zero Integration

The average GTM team runs a CRM, email platform, ad manager, analytics dashboard, content tool, outreach tool, and more — none connected. Data lives in silos. Every handoff between tools loses context, creates friction, and costs time.

Problem 02

34% of Time Wasted on Operations

CMOs spend a third of their time on coordination, reporting, and repetitive tasks that produce zero revenue. Campaign briefs take 18 hours. Board decks take a full day. A qualified lead sits untouched for 27 hours. The talent is there — the system fails them.

Problem 03

No Revenue Attribution

Most companies cannot answer: "Which campaign closed that deal?" Multi-touch attribution is technically complex and manually impossible. Every budget decision is a guess. Every channel argument is circular. Every board deck is an approximation.

Pillar 01

AI-First, Not AI-Added

Every module is built around Freya. Not a chatbot bolted on. Freya is the primary interface, the execution engine, and the intelligence layer — simultaneously active across every function.

Pillar 02

Human-in-the-Loop

Every external action passes through a configurable human approval gate. Freya does the work. Humans stay accountable. As confidence builds, autonomy increases — configured per role, per action type.

Pillar 03

Full Transparency

Every Freya output shows its reasoning. When she recommends a budget shift, she cites the data, explains the logic, and states her confidence level. Trust is built through visibility, not magic.
